Create a reusable 16:9 YouTube thumbnail blank template for a completed session of a calm personal chess livestream series.

Style:
cute, calm, airy, stationery-like, soft illustrated look, chess study diary, matcha green aesthetic.

Color palette:
The dominant palette must be pale cream, matcha green, sage green, soft dusty blue, and light beige. Brown should be minimal and never dominant. Avoid rustic wood, sepia, dark brown, orange-brown, or vintage desk aesthetics.

Overall mood:
Quiet, reflective, cute, and personal — like a cozy post-session chess diary. The image should feel like a soft stationery illustration, not a photorealistic desk photo and not an esports thumbnail.

Composition:
- A large open notebook occupying most of the image, centered or slightly left.
- A small secondary spiral notebook on the right side with a hand-drawn chessboard position and short handwritten study notes.
- A cute pale mug with matcha or coffee, in a soft cream or green tone.
- One or two subtle chess elements, such as a pawn, a knight, or a light chessboard corner.
- Small doodles and stationery details such as leaves, stars, hearts, arrows, underlines, tabs, or stickers.
- Keep the composition clean, airy, organized, and not cluttered.

Main title text:
“praticando xadrez depois dos 40”

Typography:
- “xadrez” should be the largest word.
- Use dark navy or muted blue for the main text.
- Use sage green and matcha green for accents and handwritten details.
- Keep the title block compact, visually unified, and very readable at thumbnail size.

Blank placeholders:
- “sessão #____”
- a status label reading “sessão encerrada” or “live encerrada”
- a stats area with clearly structured placeholders:
  - “duração __m”
  - “rapid ____”
  - “puzzles ____”
  - “streak __”
- a result/color badge placeholder:
  “_____ • _____”
- an opening label placeholder:
  “abertura: ____________”

Important layout guidance:
- This must clearly look like a reusable blank template.
- The placeholder zones should be clearly structured and aligned.
- Avoid ambiguous empty boxes with no obvious purpose.
- The small secondary notebook with the scribbled chessboard and study notes is required.
- The status and stats area should feel like part of a designed template, not random floating labels.
- Keep the design light and cute, with a soft matcha-green stationery feeling.

Avoid:
photorealistic dark desk, rustic wooden table, sepia tones, heavy brown palette, dramatic lighting, luxury chess set aesthetics, esports style, neon colors, cluttered composition, aggressive visual energy.
